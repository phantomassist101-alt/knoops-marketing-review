Drink-by-Drink Insights

☕

Spanish Latte — Breakout Performer

299 units, 9,966 AED. Yas Mall drove 53% of volume (158 units). At 33.3 AED/unit it's a high-margin coffee innovation.

↗ Strong candidate to become a permanent menu item.
🥥

Coconut Hot Chocolate — Premium Variant

249 units, 8,544 AED. Highest revenue per unit among drinks at 34.3 AED. Yas leads (117 units). Shows demand for premium chocolate variants with alternative milk/flavor profiles.

↗ Explore more alternative-milk/flavor pairings in the chocolate range.
🍰

Tiramisu Milkshake — Ultra-Premium

196 units, 8,026 AED. Highest ASP of any drink at 40.9 AED/unit. Evenly split between Yas (84) and MCC (80). Premium milkshake positioning is working — customers will pay 40+ AED for an indulgent experience.

↗ Use this as a proof point for future premium milkshake drops.
🌴

Date Marshmallow — Volume King, Not Revenue King

Volume king at 448 units but only 3,839 AED (8.6 AED/unit). It's a low-ASP traffic driver / add-on, not a revenue driver. MCC led with 198 units.

↗ Consider bundling it with higher-margin drinks rather than selling standalone.
🍵

Espresso Matcha — Niche but Premium

Premium at 39 AED/unit. Only 41 units — early days. Evenly spread across stores. Health-conscious positioning has potential especially with the running/cycling community near Meydan.

↗ Low volume but high ASP. Watch for growth with targeted community outreach.
🎁

Gathering Box — Premium Gifting Format

Highest ASP at 147.6 AED/unit. Only 11 sold but 1,624 AED revenue. Premium gifting format works. Meydan led (6 units).

↗ Promote more during gifting occasions — Eid, corporate orders, seasonal moments.

New Launch Spotlight: Matcha Strawberry

Matcha Strawberry — The Running Companion. Positioned as a health-forward, refreshing option targeting the active lifestyle community around DXBike Meydan and the broader fitness-conscious UAE consumer. With Espresso Matcha already showing 39 AED/unit ASP and matcha's growing popularity in the region, the Strawberry Matcha variant taps into the wellness + indulgence crossover. Key opportunity: partner with running clubs, cycling groups, and fitness events near Meydan to drive trial. This drink bridges Knoops' chocolate heritage with the health-forward trend — expanding the addressable market beyond chocolate purists.

Key Insights & Recommendations

🧊

Cold Drinks = Summer Opportunity

Cold portfolio (Iced Choc + Milkshake + Cold Coffee) grew from 19.8% of sales in Jan to 29% in Apr. Summer will push this further.

↗ Double down on cold drink marketing. Launch frozen hot chocolate at MCC & Yas.
🚀

Meydan is the Growth Engine

Consistently 120–221% over budget. Delivery-heavy location with strong neighborhood demand.

↗ Protect and increase delivery investment at Meydan. Don't reallocate budget away.
📉

Yas Mall Underperforming Budget

13–21% below budget every month. Weakest MoM recovery in April (−3.4% while others grew 40%+).

↗ Location-specific strategy needed — footfall analysis, local partnerships, Abu Dhabi-targeted marketing.
⚠️

Delivery Margins Under Pressure

CM% dropped from 34.2% to 20.0% Jan→Apr. Heavy discounting is driving volume but eroding profitability.

↗ Reduce discount dependency. Focus on menu engineering and upselling to lift AOV.
🏆

Noon is the Platform Winner

Best CM, fastest growth (+47.3%), improving CVR. Most efficient marketing spend per order across all platforms.

↗ Consider increased investment in Noon marketing spend. Test exclusive menu items.
🍫

Knoops Specials Proved the Model

~AED 25K/month since launch in March. Limited-edition and seasonal drops work — customers respond to novelty.

↗ Keep the cadence: Ramadan specials → Spring → Summer collection. Plan drops 4–6 weeks ahead.
